# MenuBox 1.5.8

MenuBox now pauses automatic hiding while you interact with menu bar icons and their menus.

- Pauses auto-hide during icon clicks and drags, and while a detected menu or popover remains open. Moving the pointer outside an open menu does not restart the timer.
- Starts the full configured delay again when the interaction ends. For example, a five-second setting waits five seconds after the menu closes.
- Applies the same countdown rules to the actual menu bar and Box UI, replacing the previous fixed minimum waits.
- Handles changes to auto-hide settings during an interaction and prevents stale timers from hiding icons after a new interaction starts.

## Known limitations

Custom popovers require recognizable macOS window or Accessibility information. Third-party apps that do not expose that information may not be detected reliably.
