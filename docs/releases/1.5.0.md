# MenuBox 1.5.0

MenuBox 1.5.0 updates settings, improves menu bar recovery, and adds opt-in crash reporting.

- Improves status-item recovery during continuous hidden-state checks.
- Uses shared settings, lifecycle, and update components from MacAppEssentials.
- Moves shortcut controls to **Keyboard Shortcuts**, and auto-hide and box-icon controls to **Display**.
- Restores **Automatically Check for Updates** in the marker's context menu.
- Adds optional Sentry crash reporting. Matching debug symbols are uploaded and verified during release preparation.
- Includes Korean and English terms under **Help & Support**, plus license and third-party notices in the app.
- Builds the layered app icon from its Icon Composer source.
- Licenses MenuBox project-owned code under GPL-3.0-only; MenuBox names, logos, and app icons remain reserved under the included brand policy.

## Known issue

The relative order of icons and the MenuBox marker can still change across app restarts or status-item re-registration. Icons to the right of the marker remain visible. Command-drag affected icons back to the intended side of the marker.
