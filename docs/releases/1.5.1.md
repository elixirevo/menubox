# MenuBox 1.5.1

MenuBox 1.5.1 adds a first-run guide and explicit agreement to the bundled terms before app features start.

- Adds onboarding for marker placement, Box UI, settings, permissions and optional crash reporting.
- Places the settings guide at step 4 and the complete localized terms at step 5.
- Requires checking the acknowledgement and choosing **Agree and Continue**. Closing before agreement quits the app.
- Keeps versioned terms acceptance separate from onboarding completion, macOS permissions and optional Sentry reporting. Existing users without a matching acceptance record see a separate agreement window.
- Starts menu bar features, shortcuts, update checking and Sentry only after agreement. Crash reporting still requires its separate opt-in.
- Adds **Show Guide** under Settings → Help & Support, without clearing existing completion or consent records.
- Uses MacAppEssentials 0.4.0 and corrects the box illustration's tape direction.

처음 실행할 때 사용법을 안내하는 온보딩을 추가했습니다. **설정 안내는 4단계**, **이용약관 동의는 5단계**이며, 체크 후 **동의하고 계속**을 눌러야 앱 기능이 시작됩니다. 기존 사용자도 해당 약관 버전의 동의 기록이 없으면 별도 동의 창을 보게 됩니다. 충돌 보고와 macOS 권한은 별도로 선택할 수 있습니다. **설정 → 도움말 및 지원 → 가이드 보기**에서 안내를 다시 볼 수 있습니다.

## Known issue

The relative order of icons and the MenuBox marker can still change across app restarts or status-item re-registration. Icons to the right of the marker remain visible. Command-drag affected icons back to the intended side of the marker.
