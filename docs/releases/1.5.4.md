# MenuBox 1.5.4

MenuBox 1.5.4 integrates MacAppEssentials 0.5.1 to improve settings and onboarding window presentation.

- Brings requested settings and onboarding windows forward and restores focus after app activation.
- Restores minimized windows while preserving MenuBox’s menu-bar-only behavior.
- Cancels delayed focus requests when a window is closed, another window is selected, or the app becomes inactive, preventing stale requests from raising a window later.

MacAppEssentials **0.5.1**을 적용했습니다. 설정·온보딩 창을 앞으로 가져오고 앱 활성화 후 포커스를 맞추도록 개선했으며, 최소화한 창의 복원을 보완했습니다. 창을 닫거나 다른 창을 선택하면 지연된 포커스 요청을 취소합니다. 기존 설정값과 메뉴바 상주 동작은 유지됩니다.

## Known issue

The relative order of icons and the MenuBox marker can still change across app restarts or status-item re-registration. Icons to the right of the marker remain visible. Command-drag affected icons back to the intended side of the marker.
